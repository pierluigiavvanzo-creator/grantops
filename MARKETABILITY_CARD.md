# MARKETABILITY_CARD.md — GrantOps

**Updated:** 2026-10-01  
**Commercial evidence level:** C0 — Hypothesis  
**Technical/product state:** local/operator MVP; not yet production SaaS-ready

## 1. ICP
Broad hypothesis: organizations or operators responsible for administering grants.  
**Exact first paying segment: UNKNOWN / not yet validated.**

## 2. Pain
Product hypothesis: grant obligations, deliverables, milestones, reporting and evidence are fragmented across grant agreements, spreadsheets, email and manual tracking, creating administrative effort and traceability risk.

The exact severity, frequency and economic cost for a chosen ICP must still be validated.

## 3. Current alternative
Current working hypothesis: manual spreadsheet/email/document workflows.  
Must be confirmed with target users before treating it as market fact.

## 4. Value proposition
Turn Grant Agreement/source material into a traceable operational workspace with:
- obligations/deliverables/milestones/reporting/evidence;
- owners, deadlines and statuses;
- source/citation/integrity provenance;
- explicit UNVERIFIED vs HUMAN_VERIFIED states;
- auditability.

Target value: lower administrative effort and lower risk of missed or weakly evidenced grant obligations.

## 5. Willingness-to-pay evidence
**NONE CANONICALLY VERIFIED YET.**

## 6. Acquisition path
**UNKNOWN.**

The first market test should choose one narrow segment and one repeatable channel. Avoid designing a broad sales organization before evidence of product value.

## 7. Time-to-value
Target first-value workflow:
`Grant Agreement/PDF -> candidate obligations -> human review -> verified operational workspace`.

Measure time from document intake to a verified workspace useful for actual grant administration.

## 8. Delivery economics
Track:
- extraction/model cost per agreement;
- human verification minutes;
- support/onboarding minutes;
- storage/monitoring cost;
- manual operations per active grant.

Do not add production infrastructure without connecting it to a customer/pilot requirement.

## 9. Compliance/security constraints
Grant documents may contain sensitive or contractual information. Production readiness must include the security/privacy controls appropriate to the selected customer and deployment model.

## 10. Differentiation hypothesis
Traceable grant-specific operations with source-backed evidence and human verification can be more trustworthy and operationally useful than generic task/project tools or document extraction alone.

Must be benchmarked against the actual alternatives used by the selected ICP.

## 11. Proof metric
Near-term product proof:
- PDF/source ingested;
- obligations extracted as candidates;
- human review completed;
- verified workspace produced;
- measurable review time and correction rate;
- useful deadlines/evidence workflow demonstrated.

Commercial proof requires a defined buyer and transaction-level evidence.

## 12. Next commercial evidence
Complete the source/PDF -> candidate obligations -> human review -> verified workspace vertical slice, then test it with a narrow real ICP and seek paid-pilot/equivalent willingness-to-pay evidence before broad SaaS expansion.

## Commercial discipline
Do not let auth, billing, tenant architecture or platform breadth become the primary roadmap until the core operator workflow and buyer value are demonstrated strongly enough to justify productionization.
