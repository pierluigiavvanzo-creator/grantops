## Product hypothesis

GrantOps is a zero-setup post-award operations workspace for EU-funded projects.

Target value hypothesis:

`Grant Agreement / source -> candidate obligations -> human verification -> verified operational workspace -> deadlines/evidence/reporting`

The intended benefit is lower administrative effort, stronger traceability and lower risk of missed or weakly evidenced obligations.

## Freeze rule

Until substantive buyer evidence exists:
- no broad SaaS platform expansion;
- no auth/billing/multi-tenant build as the primary milestone;
- no large integration program;
- no feature expansion merely because the workflow could support it;
- no scaled outreach automation.

Only the smallest product change required by a real prospect/demo/pilot may override this freeze.
