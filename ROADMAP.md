# ROADMAP.md

**Last updated:** 2026-10-01  
**Guiding metric:** `ECONOMIC VALUE × USABLE PRODUCT VALUE × AUTOMATED CUSTOMER ACQUISITION / PRODUCT OWNER TIME`

## Commercial objective

GrantOps must progress by **commercial evidence**, not by technical surface area.

Current level:

`C0 — Hypothesis`

Machine discovery has evidence. Buyer pain and willingness to pay do not.

## NOW — A Product Critical

### G1 — Substantive buyer response / problem discovery

Target:

`GRANTOPS_C1_SUBSTANTIVE_REPLY_AND_DISCOVERY`

Use the already-sent bounded outreach pilot as the current market experiment.

Success evidence is not an email delivery or automated support ticket. The useful event is a substantive response from a relevant operator/decision maker that lets us learn:
- actual painful job;
- current process/tooling;
- owner of the workflow;
- decision/budget path;
- whether a project-specific preview is worth reviewing.

No invented numeric conversion threshold is required at this stage.

### If the current pilot produces no substantive reply

Do not build more product by default.

Record:

`ICP/CHANNEL/MESSAGE_SIGNAL = WEAK_OR_UNRESOLVED`

Then redesign the cheapest next market test:
- narrower role/segment;
- better institutional route;
- clearer pain/outcome message;
- stronger project-specific proof.

A no-reply pilot is primarily evidence about the tested acquisition combination, not a final market verdict.

## NEXT — only after C1

### G2 — Project-specific solution evidence

A qualified target organisation agrees to evaluate a GrantOps preview/workflow for a real or representative EU-funded project.

Preferred evidence:
- prospect provides or authorizes use of a Grant Agreement/representative source;
- GrantOps produces candidate obligations;
- human verification completes;
- verified operational workspace is usable;
- prospect feedback identifies value, corrections, trust/security concerns and missing capabilities.

This is `C2 — Solution evidence`.

Only product gaps demonstrated by this real workflow may become Product Critical.

## THEN — transaction gate

### G3 — Economic commitment

Seek one of:
- paid bounded pilot;
- paid onboarding/workspace setup;
- signed LOI with economic commitment;
- purchase/procurement step;
- equivalent strong willingness-to-pay signal.

This is `C3 — Transaction evidence`.

Do not treat a friendly demo, generic interest or positive wording as C3.

## AFTER C3

Only after transaction evidence should the roadmap elevate:
- authentication/tenant isolation required by the buyer;
- durable production storage/backup;
- security hardening required for customer documents;
- integrations needed by the active pilot;
- billing/procurement;
- repeatable acquisition automation.

## Sellability metrics

Track:
- substantive reply classification;
- time to first substantive response;
- Product Owner/manual acquisition minutes;
- preview acceptance;
- prospect-provided document/workflow commitment;
- time from source to verified workspace;
- human verification minutes;
- correction rate;
- support/onboarding minutes;
- transaction outcome.

## Freeze list

Until C1/C2:
- generic feature expansion;
- broad integrations;
- infrastructure sophistication;
- bulk outreach automation;
- billing/multi-tenant work not required by a live pilot;
- architecture work without a buyer-facing learning objective.
