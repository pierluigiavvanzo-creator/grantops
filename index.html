2. Why you may have received a GrantOps message

We are running a small, bounded B2B validation pilot to understand whether GrantOps is useful to organisations coordinating or managing EU-funded projects.

The pilot tests a project-specific value proposition around post-award obligations, deliverables, deadlines, evidence management and reporting. It is not a mass-marketing campaign.

For the current pilot, GrantOps uses no automated follow-up, no tracking pixels, no hidden tracking identifiers and no automated decision-making about recipients.

3. What information we may process

  • organisation name and country;
  • EU-funded project identifier, acronym, title, start/end dates and coordinator role;
  • official institutional website, contact page or generic role-based mailbox;
  • professional contact information where publicly provided for an institutional or research-support function;
  • validation-contact status, including no-further-contact requests;
  • suppression information needed to respect objections or opt-outs.

We do not use guessed personal email addresses for this pilot and do not intentionally collect private, non-professional contact information.

4. Where the information comes from

The pilot uses public project and organisation information, principally from European Commission CORDIS datasets and official public institutional websites or contact pages.

If Article 14 GDPR applies because personal data were not obtained directly from you, this notice is intended to provide the relevant transparency information at or before the first communication, where required.

5. Purpose and legal basis

Purpose: to validate whether organisations responsible for EU-funded projects have a genuine need for a project-specific GrantOps post-award workspace and to evaluate the viability of the service.

Final legal basis: Legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR and, where applicable, the equivalent UK GDPR provision, subject to the electronic-marketing rules applicable to the recipient and channel.

If legitimate interests are used, the controller's documented interest and balancing rationale is:

GrantOps has a legitimate commercial interest in validating demand for a post-award operations service among organisations that publicly act as coordinators or managers of EU-funded projects. The pilot is limited to publicly available project information and official institutional or role-based contact routes, one initial professionally relevant communication per organisation, no automated follow-up, no tracking, clear identification of the sender, an immediate no-further-contact route, and a suppression list to respect objections. The processing is therefore intentionally narrow and proportionate to the validation purpose.

Applicable electronic-marketing rules are separate from the GDPR lawful-basis analysis. GrantOps only intends to use a route where the communication is permitted under the rules applicable to that recipient and channel.

6. How we contact organisations

  • no automated follow-up;
  • no tracking pixels or hidden identifiers;
  • no attachment required to understand or reject the message;
  • a clear route to request no further contact.
